## Becoming a maintainer

We are actively looking to grow the maintainer group so the project
does not depend on one person or one timezone. If you have a track
record of quality contributions and reviews, express interest in an
issue — see the
[contributor → maintainer path](GOVERNANCE.md#path-from-contributor-to-maintainer).
